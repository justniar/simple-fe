About Cirebon

Cirebon, a coastal city on the northern coast of Java, Indonesia, is known for its rich cultural heritage, historic palaces, and delicious cuisine. The city blends Javanese, Sundanese, Chinese, and Islamic influences, making it a unique travel destination.

Famous for its batik industry, Cirebon offers stunning traditional patterns like "Mega Mendung." Visitors can explore places like Keraton Kasepuhan, Sunyaragi Cave, and taste local delicacies such as Nasi Jamblang and Empal Gentong.

Travel Tips

The best time to visit Cirebon is during the dry season from May to September.

Use becak (pedicab), angkot (minibus), or rent a motorcycle for easy travel.

Don't miss trying Nasi Jamblang, Empal Gentong, and Tahu Gejrot.

Plan your budget and look for discounts before booking flights and hotels.
